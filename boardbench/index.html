04 Pinout 101

Three rules that save magic-smoke moments.

A

Match your voltage

5 V logic (classic Arduino) will fry 3.3 V boards (Pico, ESP32). Check both boards before you share a wire — level shifters are cheap insurance.

B

Share the ground

Signals are a comparison, not a monologue. Every module needs a common GND with your board or the data line is just noise.

C

Respect the pins

PWM, ADC, I²C, SPI — each pin has a day job. Read the pinout diagram before wiring; GPIO 2 is not just "pin number two".
